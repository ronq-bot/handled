## Description

The monsters are marching on the stage — and only the Idol Squad can stop them!

Lead a team of pop-star monster hunters in this tower defense shooter. Your idols fire on their own; your job is to keep them loaded. Swipe across the ammo belt to SLICE bullets into ammo — snick, snick, SNICK — and watch it fly straight to whoever needs it most.

SLICE, SHOOT, SURVIVE
• Super-satisfying swipe-to-slice ammo belt — watch for golden bullets!
• Idols fire automatically — you decide where the power goes
• Pick one of three upgrade cards every level-up and build a new combo every run

MEET THE SQUAD
• RIYA — rapid-fire Mic Blaster
• KAI — Bass Bazooka with splash damage
• LUNA — Lightstick Laser that pierces every monster in a line
• DAE — Freeze Beat that slows monsters to a shuffle
• SORA — Thunder Fan with chain lightning

THE WEIRDEST MONSTERS AROUND
Bonkito the bat-swinging log, Sharky Kicks, Latte Ballerina, the Crocopter, Cactus Elefanto and a horde of toothy Gremlos — plus giant bosses with signature moves: Mega Bonkito's BONK RUSH, King Gremlo's minion army and the meatball-flinging Spaghetti Kraken.

THREE WORLDS, 30 AREAS
Brainrot Beach, Toybox Town and Cosmic Disco — each tougher and stranger than the last. Clear Area 5 to unlock SURVIVAL MODE and see how long you can hold the stage.

LEVEL UP YOUR SQUAD
Earn coins every run to unlock idols, level them up and buy permanent boosts. Come back each day for a free daily gift.

• Play offline — no internet needed
• No ads. No tracking. No accounts.

gambling, loot boxes (upgrade cards are free in-run choices, not purchases),
