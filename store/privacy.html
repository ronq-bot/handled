Privacy Policy

Idol Squad: Brainrot Defense — last updated October 2026.

Idol Squad does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • There are no accounts, logins or sign-ups.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • The game makes no network requests. It works fully offline.
  • Your game progress (coins, unlocked idols, settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Because no data is collected, the game is safe for players of all ages, including children, and no data can be requested, sold or deleted on our side.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page before any update that changes it is released.
