How do I get more ammo?

Swipe across the ammo belt at the bottom of the screen to slice bullets. Each sliced bullet flies to the idol with the emptiest magazine. Golden bullets refill the whole squad!

I'm stuck on an area.

Every run earns coins, even when you lose. Spend them on the SQUAD tab to level up idols, or on BOOST for permanent upgrades. Unlocking a third idol helps a lot.

How do I unlock Survival mode?

Clear Area 5.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Progress is stored on your device only. Restoring a device backup (iCloud or computer) restores your progress.

How do I start over?

Tap the gear icon on the home screen, then RESET PROGRESS and tap it again to confirm.
